# Village Defense & Raids smoke test — Minecraft 26.1

## Preparation

1. Start a world with cheats enabled, two nearby hired guards, and a village suitable for a vanilla raid.
2. Keep one hired guard loaded and move the other far enough away to unload its chunk.
3. Set Raid Behavior to `Normal` from the Quick Command panel (`G`).
4. Start a vanilla raid while the commander is within 128 blocks of its center.

## Opportunity and validation

1. Confirm that a compact `Village Under Attack` alert appears once for the raid and reports only loaded guards.
2. Dismiss the alert with `Esc`; confirm it behaves as `Ignore` and does not reopen for the same active raid.
3. Start another raid or repeat in a fresh world, choose `Rally guards`, and confirm only loaded guards owned by that player are summoned.
4. Repeat with `Defend village`; confirm guards receive distinct safe posts near the raid center and remain within the 16-block defense radius.
5. Confirm short/tall grass and dirt paths do not make otherwise safe posts unavailable; water, fire, cactus, magma, powder snow and obstructed spaces must still be rejected.
6. Confirm defenders run toward their assigned posts at the same moderate catch-up pace used while following a sprinting owner.
7. Before the raid, give different guards a route, a fixed patrol point, a follow order, and no task; finish or cancel the raid and confirm each resumes its exact previous task after the report appears.
8. Move beyond 128 blocks or change dimension before responding and confirm the stale response is rejected without issuing orders.
9. Confirm no unloaded chunk is loaded and the unloaded guard is never teleported or ordered.

## Persistent behavior

1. In the Quick Command panel, set Raid Behavior to `Rally to Village` and reconnect or reload the world.
2. Confirm the setting remains selected and the next eligible raid applies village defense without opening the alert.
3. Change the setting to `Rally to Commander`; confirm the next eligible raid uses the existing summon behavior.
4. Restore `Normal`; confirm later raids show the alert again.

## Report

1. Defend a raid and let hired guards contribute damage to at least two raiders; have one guard land a confirmed final blow.
2. If practical, let one loaded hired guard die during the active report session.
3. Finish or stop the raid and confirm one report appears with deployed guards, observed assisted defeats, loaded losses, and the confirmed-kill MVP.
4. Repeat while unloading a participating guard and confirm the report is explicitly marked partial rather than estimating missing data.
5. Confirm the report waits until other open GUI screens are closed instead of interrupting them.

## Multiplayer isolation

1. Join with two players, each owning at least one loaded guard near the same raid.
2. Confirm both players receive independent opportunities.
3. Have one player choose `Ignore` and the other choose `Defend village`; confirm neither choice closes or changes the other player's session.
4. Finish the raid and confirm each player receives only their own deployment, contribution, loss, and MVP data.
