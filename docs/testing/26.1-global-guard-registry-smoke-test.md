# Global Guard Registry smoke test — Minecraft 26.1

## Preparation

1. Start a world with at least two hired guards.
2. Give the guards distinct names and put one in a Squad.
3. Open the Commander's Ledger and select `Global registry`.

## Loaded records

1. Confirm that both guards appear with name, rank, Squad, dimension, coordinates and `Loaded`.
2. Move a guard and change its order.
3. Wait at least five seconds, reopen the registry and confirm position and order were updated.
4. Confirm that distance appears only for guards in the player's current dimension.

## Unloaded records

1. Move far enough to unload a guard's chunk without killing it.
2. Reopen the registry and confirm the guard remains listed as `Chunk unloaded`.
3. Save and reload the world; confirm the unloaded record and its last known position remain.
4. Load the guard again and confirm the entry returns to `Loaded` with current data.

## Lifecycle

1. Hire a new guard and confirm it enters the registry immediately.
2. Trigger a failed recruitment rollback and confirm no stale record remains.
3. Kill a hired guard and confirm its record is removed.
4. Run `/rallyguard registry count` and compare the result with the screen.
5. Run `/rallyguard registry refresh` and confirm loaded guards are reconciled.

## Paging and safety

1. Test with more than eight registered guards and navigate between server pages.
2. Confirm the registry never loads an unloaded chunk or teleports a guard.
3. Confirm no guard owned by another player appears.
4. Confirm unloaded rows expose no command buttons.
