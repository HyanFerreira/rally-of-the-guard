# Minecraft 26.1 — Squads & Officers Smoke Test

Use a disposable world with two players when multiplayer checks are possible.
Keep one hired Captain, two ordinary hired guards, and one unowned guard nearby.

## Automated prerequisites

- [ ] `./gradlew.bat test` reports `BUILD SUCCESSFUL`.
- [ ] `./gradlew.bat build` reports `BUILD SUCCESSFUL`.
- [ ] `git diff --check` reports no whitespace errors.

## Squad lifecycle

- [ ] Open the Commander's Ledger and create `Alpha Squad`.
- [ ] Create `Bravo Squad`; confirm both remain ordered after closing and reopening the world.
- [ ] Rename `Bravo Squad` and confirm the updated label appears in the Ledger scope selector.
- [ ] Confirm blank, duplicate-case, over-limit, and over-length names are rejected without mutation.
- [ ] Delete a Squad, cancel once, then confirm; loaded members become Unassigned and retain their current orders.
- [ ] Disable Squads in configuration; confirm management, scope, and collective controls disappear, existing data survives, and manually sent mutation/collective packets are rejected. Re-enable it.

## Membership and persistence

- [ ] Confirm the left column lists Unassigned guards and members of other Squads with their current Squad below the name.
- [ ] Confirm the right column lists only Alpha members and that names, ranks, and membership remain readable while rows are hovered or selected.
- [ ] Assign two hired guards to Alpha with the right arrow and confirm a guard cannot belong to two Squads simultaneously.
- [ ] Move one guard from Alpha to Bravo, confirm the transfer dialog names both Squads, then confirm both columns update from the server snapshot.
- [ ] Unassign a guard and confirm it appears under Unassigned.
- [ ] Attempt assignment with the second player and confirm ownership denial.
- [ ] Assign a guard, unload its chunk, delete the Squad, then reload the guard; confirm lazy repair to Unassigned.
- [ ] Save and reload the world; confirm valid assignments persist.

## Officers

- [ ] Assign the Captain to Alpha and set it as Officer.
- [ ] Confirm an ordinary guard cannot become Officer.
- [ ] Replace or clear the Officer and confirm the previous guard remains a Captain in the Muster Roll.
- [ ] Move the active Officer to another Squad and confirm Alpha's Officer clears automatically.
- [ ] Delete Alpha and confirm the Captain status remains unchanged.

## Scoped orders

- [ ] Select Alpha and issue collective Summon, Follow, Wait, and Patrol; only loaded Alpha members respond.
- [ ] Select Unassigned and repeat the commands; only unassigned guards respond.
- [ ] Select All Guards and confirm all loaded owned guards in the dimension respond.
- [ ] After a collective Follow order, confirm the affected row states update after the server snapshot without reopening the Ledger.
- [ ] Put one member in another dimension or unload it and confirm it is not listed or commanded.
- [ ] Confirm individual actions and route editing still work in every filtered view.
- [ ] Issue combat orders from Alpha, Unassigned, and All Guards; only ready rallied/following guards in that scope attack.

## UI and localization

- [ ] Navigate the Ledger and Squad management screen at a narrow supported window size without clipped essential controls.
- [ ] Confirm each Ledger roster row shows the guard's name, rank/settlement metadata, and current Squad or Unassigned in every filter.
- [ ] Confirm selecting a roster row updates the single Individual Orders panel and does not emit an order by itself.
- [ ] Confirm Individual Orders affect only the selected guard and Orders for the current scope affect the entire visible scope.
- [ ] Confirm create, rename, delete confirmation, assignment, Officer, paging, and Back controls work with mouse input.
- [ ] Switch between English and Brazilian Portuguese and confirm no raw Squad translation keys are visible.
