# Command Post smoke test — Minecraft 26.1

## Preparation

1. Start a world with Rally of the Guard and its required dependencies.
2. Hire at least three guards; put one of them in a Squad.
3. Obtain or craft a Command Post.

## Placement and ownership

1. Place the Command Post and right-click it with an empty hand.
2. Confirm that the management screen opens for the placing player.
3. Confirm that another player cannot edit the post.
4. Break the post and confirm that it drops itself.

## Management screen

1. Confirm that available guards appear in the left column and assigned defenders in the right.
2. Confirm that each guard shows its name and Squad, or `No Squad`.
3. Rename the post, change radius and cycle the formation; close and reopen the screen.
4. Confirm that all settings survived the reload.

## Territorial defense

1. Assign at least three guards and confirm that they receive distinct formation posts.
2. Lure a hostile mob inside the radius and confirm normal Guard Villagers combat.
3. Lure the target outside the radius and confirm that guards disengage and return.
4. Disable the post and confirm that only defense orders originating from this post are cleared.
5. Re-enable the post and confirm that its defenders resume territorial defense.
6. Give one defender a manual order and confirm that the periodic post update does not overwrite it.

## Transfer and persistence

1. Place a second post and assign a defender from the first post.
2. Confirm the transfer warning and accept it.
3. Confirm that the second post controls the defender and the first does not reclaim it.
4. Save and reload the world; confirm post name, settings and assignments remain intact.
